# Motor Comprehensive

Master wording for the ClaimGuard reference motor product. Clause IDs are cited
verbatim in adjudication decisions, so they must not be renumbered.

## MC-1.1 Scope of cover
This policy indemnifies the insured against accidental loss of, or damage to, the
insured vehicle described in the schedule, occurring within the territorial limits
during the period of insurance. Cover extends to fire, self-ignition, lightning,
explosion, burglary, housebreaking, theft, riot, strike, malicious act, terrorist
activity, and damage in transit by road, rail, inland waterway, lift or elevator.

## MC-1.2 Period of insurance
Cover begins at the inception date and time shown in the schedule and ends at the
expiry date and time. A loss occurring before inception or after expiry is not
payable, irrespective of when it is reported. Where a policy has been reinstated
after lapse, losses occurring during the lapsed interval are not covered.

## MC-1.3 Sum insured and limit of liability
The maximum amount payable for any one claim is the Insured Declared Value shown
in the schedule, less the applicable deductible. Where repair cost exceeds 75% of
the Insured Declared Value, the vehicle may be treated as a constructive total
loss and settled on a total-loss basis.

## MC-1.4 Deductible
Every claim is subject to the compulsory deductible stated in the schedule. The
deductible is applied once per claim event, not per damaged part. A voluntary
deductible, where elected, is applied in addition to the compulsory deductible.

## MC-2.1 Own damage
Damage to the insured vehicle arising from collision, overturning, impact with a
fixed or moving object, or the negligent act of a third party, is payable subject
to the deductible and the limit of liability. Depreciation is applied to plastic,
rubber, nylon and fibreglass parts at the rates set out in the schedule.

## MC-2.2 Theft of vehicle
Total theft of the insured vehicle is payable on production of a police report
and surrender of both sets of keys. A theft claim without a First Information
Report, or where only one key can be produced, is referred for investigation
before settlement.

## MC-2.3 Fire and explosion
Loss or damage by fire, self-ignition, lightning or explosion is payable. Damage
caused by a fire resulting from an unauthorised after-market electrical
modification is excluded under MC-3.6.

## MC-2.4 Glass and windscreen
Standalone breakage of windscreen, window or sunroof glass is payable without
affecting the no-claim bonus, provided no other part of the vehicle is damaged in
the same event. Claims for glass damage accompanied by body damage are treated as
ordinary own-damage claims.

## MC-2.5 Weather and natural perils
Damage caused by flood, inundation, storm, tempest, hurricane, cyclone,
hailstorm, landslide or rockslide is payable. Engine damage caused by attempting
to start a vehicle after water ingress is excluded under MC-3.5.

## MC-2.6 Third party injury
Legal liability for death or bodily injury to a third party is covered to the
statutory limit. Any injury claim requires a medical report and, where the
incident was attended by police, the corresponding report.

## MC-3.1 General exclusions
The policy does not cover consequential loss, depreciation beyond the schedule
rates, wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure or breakage, or
damage to tyres and tubes unless the vehicle is damaged at the same time.

## MC-3.2 Driving without a valid licence
No claim is payable where, at the time of the incident, the vehicle was being
driven by a person not holding a valid and effective driving licence for that
class of vehicle.

## MC-3.3 Driving under the influence
No claim is payable where the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs
at the time of the incident.

## MC-3.4 Use outside the declared purpose
Cover is limited to the use stated in the schedule. Loss occurring while the
vehicle is used for hire or reward, racing, speed testing, reliability trials, or
any purpose connected with the motor trade, is excluded unless specifically
endorsed.

## MC-3.5 Water ingress
Damage to the engine or transmission caused by hydrostatic lock, or by any
attempt to start or run the vehicle after the ingress of water, is excluded
unless an engine protection endorsement is in force.

## MC-3.6 Unauthorised modification
Loss or damage attributable to an after-market modification not declared to and
accepted by the insurer, including electrical, fuel-system and structural
modifications, is excluded.

## MC-3.7 Deliberate act
No claim is payable where the loss was caused by a wilful or deliberate act of the
insured, a member of the insured's household, or a person acting with their
knowledge or consent.

## MC-4.1 Duty to notify
The insured must notify the insurer of any incident likely to give rise to a claim
as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event within thirty days. Late
notification does not automatically void a claim, but the insurer may reduce
settlement to the extent that the delay has prejudiced its ability to investigate.

## MC-4.2 Duty to preserve evidence
The insured must not commence repairs, dispose of damaged parts, or alter the
vehicle's condition before the insurer has had a reasonable opportunity to
inspect, except where necessary to prevent further loss or to comply with the law.

## MC-4.3 Documentation required
A claim must be supported by the claim form, a copy of the registration
certificate, the driving licence of the person driving at the time, a repair
estimate from the attending garage, the final invoice, and photographs of the
damage. Injury claims additionally require medical documentation.

## MC-4.4 Fraud
If a claim is in any respect fraudulent, or if any fraudulent means or devices are
used to obtain benefit under this policy, all benefit under the policy is
forfeited and the insurer may declare the policy void from the date of the
fraudulent act. This clause applies to inflated invoices, staged incidents,
pre-existing damage presented as new, and claims filed in collusion with repairers
or other claimants.

## MC-4.5 Contribution and other insurance
Where the same loss is covered by another policy, the insurer's liability is
limited to its rateable proportion. A claim recovered in full from another insurer
is not payable again under this policy.

## MC-5.1 No-claim bonus
The no-claim bonus shown in the schedule is forfeited on any claim other than a
standalone glass claim under MC-2.4. The bonus-malus level is recalculated at
renewal.

## MC-5.2 Settlement basis
Repairable damage is settled on a repair basis using the insurer's approved parts
and labour rates. Where repairs are carried out at a non-network garage, the
insurer settles at the equivalent network rate. Salvage remains the property of
the insurer on total-loss settlements.
