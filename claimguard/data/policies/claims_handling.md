# Claims Handling Manual

Internal handling rules the adjudication agent applies alongside the policy
wording. These are operational standards, not contract terms.

## CH-1.1 Triage bands
Claims are triaged into three bands on the blended fraud score. Below 0.25 the
claim is straight-through processed. Between 0.25 and 0.85 it is routed to a
human adjudicator with the evidence pack attached. At or above 0.85 it is
referred to the Special Investigation Unit before any payment is made.

## CH-1.2 High-value ceiling
No claim above the high-value ceiling set in configuration is straight-through
processed, regardless of fraud score. High-value claims always receive a human
sign-off.

## CH-2.1 Indicators of inflated repair cost
Treat as an inflated-cost indicator: an invoice more than 15% above the original
estimate without a documented supplementary approval; parts billed that are not
visible in the damage photographs; labour hours exceeding the manufacturer's
standard time by more than 30%; the same invoice number appearing on more than
one claim.

## CH-2.2 Indicators of a staged incident
Treat as staged-incident indicators: no independent witnesses and no police
attendance on a high-value claim; the incident occurring within thirty days of
policy inception or within thirty days of a cover upgrade; damage patterns
inconsistent with the described mechanism of impact; all occupants claiming
soft-tissue injury with the same clinic.

## CH-2.3 Indicators of organised fraud
Treat as organised-fraud indicators: multiple unrelated claimants sharing a
telephone number, bank account or address; a single repairer or clinic appearing
across an unusually high share of a claimant cluster; sequential claims in the
same postcode within a short window with similar damage profiles; vehicles that
change ownership shortly before a claim.

## CH-3.1 Evidence pack
Every referred claim must carry the retrieved policy clauses relied on, the
document mismatches found, the damage findings, the model's top contributing
features, and the graph neighbourhood. A referral without an evidence pack is
returned to the queue.

## CH-3.2 Explainability standard
A decline must cite the clause it rests on and the specific evidence that
triggered it. A fraud score alone is never sufficient grounds to decline a claim;
it is grounds to investigate.

## CH-3.3 Recovery and reserve
Where a claim is referred, the reserve is held at the claimed amount until the
investigation closes. Where fraud is established, recovery action is opened
against every linked claim in the same cluster, not only the claim that triggered
the investigation.

## CH-4.1 Turnaround targets
Straight-through processed claims settle within two working days. Referred claims
receive a first investigator contact within five working days. SIU referrals are
acknowledged within one working day.

## CH-4.2 Customer communication
Every decision letter states the outcome, the clause or handling rule relied on,
the amount payable, and the route to appeal. Letters must be free of internal
scoring language: the customer is told what was found, not what the model scored.
